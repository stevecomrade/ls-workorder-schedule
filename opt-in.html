Comrade Cycles — SMS Consent Documentation

Toll-Free Verification Resubmission  ·  Error 30511 Response  ·  Verbal Consent Script & Procedures

Business: Comrade Cycles  |  1908 W. Chicago Ave, Chicago IL 60622  |  comradecycles.com  |  Use case: Repair status & appointment notifications

Resubmission reason: Rejection under Twilio error 30511 — verbal consent script not provided

Use case: Transactional SMS notifications for bicycle repair customers (work order status, ready notifications)

Message volume: Low — typically 1–3 messages per work order per customer

Sender: Comrade Cycles toll-free number via Twilio

1  ·  Verbal Consent Script REQUIRED

The following is the exact language used by staff when collecting a customer's phone number at drop-off or check-in:

"We'll send you a text message when your bike is ready and if we have any questions during the repair. Standard message rates may apply. Is that okay?"

No messages are sent to the customer's number until verbal consent is confirmed and the work order is created in the system.

2  ·  Simulated Consent Conversation REQUIRED

The following demonstrates a typical intake interaction at the shop counter:

Staff: "Can I get a phone number to reach you about your repair?"

Customer: "Sure, it's 312-555-0187."

Staff: "Great. We'll send you a text when your bike is ready and if we have any questions. Standard message rates may apply — is that okay?"

Customer: "Yes, that's fine."

[Staff records phone number and creates work order in Lightspeed Retail POS. Consent is captured at this step.]

If the customer declines, no phone number is recorded and no messages are sent.

3  ·  How Consent Is Recorded & Stored REQUIRED

Verbal consent is recorded at the time of drop-off in the customer's work order inside Lightspeed Retail POS. Each work order captures:

Field Value / Description
Customer nameFull name as provided at intake
Phone numberMobile number provided verbally by customer
Work order numberUnique system-generated identifier
Date & timeAutomatically timestamped at work order creation
Staff memberLogged-in employee who created the work order
SMS Consent fieldChecked by staff at intake to confirm verbal consent was obtained

Records are retained for a minimum of 2 years per standard business recordkeeping. No messages are sent to any number unless a work order with confirmed consent exists in the system.

4  ·  Opt-Out Instructions REQUIRED

Customers can opt out at any time using any of the following methods:

  • Reply STOP to any message received — immediately removes the number from all future messages
  • Reply HELP to receive contact information for the shop
  • Call or visit the shop in person and request removal

STOP replies are handled automatically by Twilio. Once received, no further messages are sent to that number. Opt-out is honored immediately and permanently unless the customer re-consents at a future visit.

5  ·  Confirmation Text After Verbal Opt-In IMPLEMENTED

Upon creation of a work order with a customer phone number, the following confirmation message is sent automatically before any other messages:

Comrade Cycles: You're confirmed for text updates on your repair. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for info. Msg & data rates may apply.

This confirmation serves as written acknowledgment of the verbal consent collected at intake and provides the customer an immediate opt-out opportunity before any further messages are sent.

Subsequent messages are limited to:

  • Status updates if a question arises during the repair
  • Notification that the bike is ready for pickup
